<div class="home-intro">
  <p class="home-kicker">Agentic AI &middot; Efficient Learning &middot; Robust Machine Learning</p>
  <p class="home-lead">I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Emory University, advised by <a href="https://cs.emory.edu/~lzhao41/#students">Liang Zhao</a>. I study how intelligent systems learn, coordinate, and remain reliable as models, memory, and agent networks scale.</p>
  <p>My work draws on machine learning, statistics, optimization, and complex systems. Before joining Emory, I trained in statistics at the National University of Singapore and in physics and mathematics at Peking University, and worked as a machine learning engineer at Alibaba Group.</p>
</div>
